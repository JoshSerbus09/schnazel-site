# Privacy Policy for Schnazel

**Effective date:** 2 October 2026
**Developer:** Serbus Game Studios
**Contact:** SerbusGameStudios@gmail.com

This policy explains how the Schnazel mobile game ("the app") handles your information.

## The short version

Schnazel does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The app has no
user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network features. The app itself sends
nothing off your device.

## Information we collect

**None.** We do not collect, request, or receive any personal information about you. The
app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos,
or any other personal data, and it does not read any of it in the background.

We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services, and we do not use cookies, tracking
pixels, advertising identifiers, or any similar tracking technology.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally so it can remember your preferences between
sessions:

- **Game settings** — your chosen player count, opponent difficulty, visual style, game
  speed, and similar gameplay preferences.
- **Game statistics** — your lifetime record of games played and won.

This data is stored in the app's private storage on your own device. The app never
transmits it to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to see it. It is removed from the
device when you uninstall the app. You can also clear your statistics at any time from
within the app.

**Android device backup.** The app allows Android's built-in backup, so that your settings
and statistics can be restored if you reinstall the app or move to a new phone. If backup
is turned on for your device, Android saves only those two small files to your own Google
account and restores them for you. That is a feature of your device and your Google account, governed by
Google's terms and your device's backup settings; it is not sent to us and we cannot access
it. You can turn it off in your Android settings.

## Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose any information to third parties, because we do
not collect any.

## Children

Schnazel is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal
information from anyone, it collects none from children.

## Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage,
or any other sensitive device permission.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change — for example, if a future version adds advertising or
online play — we will update this policy and revise the effective date above before those
features are released. Continued use of the app after a change means the updated policy
applies.

If you have questions about this policy, email **SerbusGameStudios@gmail.com**.
